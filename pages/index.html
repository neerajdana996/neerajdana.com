The problems teams usually bring me

If one of these sounds familiar, the fix is rarely another retry or a longer timeout. It's usually a missing guarantee somewhere in the design.

  • Messages get processed twice after deploys or rebalances.Duplicate charges, duplicate emails, counters that drift. I trace where at-least-once delivery meets a non-idempotent side effect.
  • Consumer lag climbs and nobody can say why.Slow handlers, poison messages, rebalance storms or a hot partition. I find which one and fix the cause, not the alert.
  • Two systems are "in sync" until a user notices they aren't.Bidirectional sync across different data and permission models, with conflict rules and a reconcile loop that catches drift.
  • The cache made things fast and occasionally wrong.Invalidation tied to events instead of timers, measured at p95 and p99 rather than averages.
  • Your LLM feature works in the demo and breaks on real inputs.Orchestration, output validation and evals that tell you what failed and why, before your users do.
  • End-to-end tests fail at random and the team ignores CI.Flakiness is usually a concurrency bug in the tests or the product. I classify the failures and remove the causes.
